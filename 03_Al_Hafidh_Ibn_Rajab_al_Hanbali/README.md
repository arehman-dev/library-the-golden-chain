# Al-Hafidh Zayn al-Din Abd al-Rahman ibn Ahmad Ibn Rajab al-Hanbali
### زين الدين عبد الرحمن بن أحمد بن رجب الحنبلي
**Dates**: 736–795 AH / 1335–1393 CE  
**Lineage**: The Golden Chain of Hanbali scholarship (*Al-Silsilah al-Dhahabiyyah*)  
**Biography**: Distinguished Hanbali hadith master, jurist, and ascetic, student of Ibn al-Qayyim, famed for Jami' al-'Ulum wal-Hikam and Lata'if al-Ma'arif.
