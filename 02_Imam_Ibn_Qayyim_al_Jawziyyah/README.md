# Imam Shams al-Din Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyyah
### محمد بن أبي بكر بن أيوب شمس الدين ابن قيم الجوزية
**Dates**: 691–751 AH / 1292–1350 CE  
**Lineage**: The Golden Chain of Hanbali scholarship (*Al-Silsilah al-Dhahabiyyah*)  
**Biography**: Foremost disciple of Ibn Taymiyyah, renowned jurist, spiritual doctor of hearts (tazkiyah), and master of prophetic biography and usool.
